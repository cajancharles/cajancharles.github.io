FAQ

Quick answers.

Which businesses can use it?

Carwash, cafe, carinderia, retail shop and sari-sari store out of the box. Salons, barbershops, restaurants and other small businesses can be set up too. Just message me.

Is it really a one-time payment?

Yes. You pay once for a lifetime license. No monthly fees, per-branch fees or renewals.

Is the ₱4,500 price permanent?

No. ₱4,500 (down from ₱10,000) is a limited-time offer. After it ends, the regular ₱10,000 price applies.

Can I try it first?

Yes. The live demo is a carwash setup on randomized sample data. Click through checkout, inventory, payroll and reports yourself.

Can I change items, prices and commissions?

Yes. Everything is editable in your own setup: items, variants, packages, add-ons, prices and commission rates.

Are receipts official BIR receipts?

No. Receipts are sales invoices for internal record-keeping. Owners remain responsible for complying with tax and business regulations.
